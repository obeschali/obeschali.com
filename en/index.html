01

Promised. is a project about public statements and their real-world consequences. Each story begins with a promise, forecast, or assurance; then we look at what happened.

02

Some statements can be checked against documents and figures. Others need context. That is why each story includes sources: you can see for yourself who said what, and what we compare it with.

03

This is an author-curated selection, not a complete catalogue of every promise. It brings together stories that seemed important and can be documented. If we got something wrong or missed context, you can write to us.

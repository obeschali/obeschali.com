# Editorial and verification notes

The archive contains English localization for all 81 stories, the 141-entry glossary, all UI strings, and all seven About-screen strings. Translations preserve the original Russian JSON fields, identifiers, dates, source URLs, source ordering, and hashes. No additional English source URLs were added to story records.

## Cases to keep in view

- `putin-2014-krym-samooborona`: The original quote includes the bracketed editorial clarification “[Крыма]”; the English keeps it as “[in Crimea].” The other bracketed insertion in the story’s outcome is translated as “the question of Crimea’s accession.”
- `mutko-2017-doping`: The quote is excerpted with an initial ellipsis and an editorial bracket. “State-run” is a contextual rendering of “государственная”; the full recording should govern if a verbatim quotation is later prepared.
- `antonov-2022-ne-planiruetsya`: The quote is fragmentary and contains two ellipses. They are retained. The official phrase “special military operation” is presented as a quoted Russian label in the outcome.
- `putin-2022-okkupatsiya`: “People’s republics” is used for the self-designated entities; the text also makes clear that Ukraine and other countries did not recognize the referendums.
- `putin-2014-donbass`: The later statement is a separate, longer excerpt; it should not be read as a verbatim translation of the earlier denial.
- `putin-2021-dvorets`: “Beneficiary” preserves the distinction between a reported beneficial interest and legal ownership. The outcome explicitly states that the two statements are not a direct contradiction.
- `putin-2020-navalny`: The bracketed “[kill him]” supplies an omitted object. The story does not infer or state a cause of death.
- `siluanov-2016-pensii`: The quotation is context-poor in isolation. Check its antecedent in the original source before reuse as a standalone quotation.
- `peskov-2024-youtube`: The outcome distinguishes slowing a service from blocking it.
- `press-sluzhb-telegram`: “At present” is retained because it limits the scope of the original assurance.
- `putin-2012-rozhdaemost`, `putin-2018-prodolzhitelnost-zhizni`, and the fiscal/economic projections: The English preserves dates, units, forecast-versus-outcome distinctions, and the stated source limitations.
- `er-2006-dtp`: The Russian “в 1,5 раза” is adapted as “to two-thirds” to express the intended 1.5-fold reduction without the misleading English construction “1.5 times fewer.”
- `slyusar-2019-su57`, `chemezov-2022-ssj100`, and `putin-2012-rabochie-mesta`: The source itself says the later delivery/final result was not checked or was not available; that limitation is retained.
- `dvorkovich-2008-devalvatsiya`: The Russian record contains no place or event context. The English `meta_context` remains empty for the same reason; no context was invented.
